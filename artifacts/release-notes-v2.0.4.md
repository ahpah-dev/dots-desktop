Your dot can keep you company while it works.

- Polished activity animations for writing and reading files, web searches, commands, memory, and connected tools.
- A small, draggable desktop dot shares live updates when you minimize Dots or close it to the system tray. It follows active work and approvals automatically.
- Open the right conversation, stop the current task, switch teammates, or hide the dot directly from its speech bubble.
- Settings → Desktop offers an always-visible mode and optional spoken updates. Voice is off by default, and the dot remembers where you move it.
- Lightweight CSS motion pauses when hidden and respects reduced-motion preferences. The companion exposes only its own limited controls and receives activity summaries without tool arguments or output.

Install over your current version to keep your dots, settings, conversations, and workspaces. Dots continues working while it runs in the tray; fully quitting stops tasks and closes the desktop dot.

Windows x64 installer and portable builds are included with SHA256 checksums.

Validation: TypeScript production build, activity selection and settings persistence tests, real two-window Electron activity/control/lifecycle checks, packaged app motion and full workflow checks, and website playback, keyboard, responsive, and reduced-motion checks.
