Edit your own sent messages and restart a conversation from an earlier point.

- Click **Edit** beneath your message, change it, then **Save & resend**. Escape or Cancel closes the editor without changing anything.
- Click **Revert here** to resend the original message from that point.
- Earlier conversation turns are retained in the new branch. Later turns are excluded from both displayed history and provider context. The original conversation stays available under **Conversations**.
- Finish or stop active work before revising. Conversation revisions do not undo workspace files, saved memories, or scheduled work.
- The updated four-dot logo remains included in the desktop shortcut and application icons.

Install over your existing version to keep local data. Windows x64 installer and portable builds are included, with SHA256SUMS.

Validation: TypeScript and production build, engine/storage tests, actual Electron UI edit/revert and IPC/provider context checks, and packaged app smoke checks.
